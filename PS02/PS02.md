## Domain

Financial Technology, Accounting Automation, and Data Reconciliation


## Problem Statement

Small businesses often maintain invoices and payment records in separate spreadsheets or systems. Differences in customer names, missing invoice references, repeated payment amounts, and unclear transaction descriptions can make it difficult to determine which invoices have been paid and which still require attention.

Develop a software application that imports invoice and payment records from CSV files and suggests matches between them. The system should use relevant evidence—such as invoice references, customer identifiers, amounts, and transaction dates—to identify likely relationships. Each suggestion must include an understandable explanation of why the records were matched.

Users should be able to review, confirm, reject, or correct suggested matches. Ambiguous cases and amount mismatches must remain visible for investigation. The application should calculate outstanding balances using confirmed allocations and prevent the same payment amount from being counted more than once.

For the 24-hour prototype, use a supplied dataset in a single currency and demonstrate the complete workflow from file import to reconciliation report. Partial-payment aggregation, duplicate detection, and fuzzy matching of unclear descriptions may be implemented as extensions.

## Key Deliverable

A working reconciliation dashboard that allows users to:

- Upload invoice and payment CSV files and identify invalid or incomplete records.
- View suggested matches with supporting evidence and a confidence indicator.
- Confirm, reject, or manually assign matches.
- Inspect unmatched invoices, unallocated payments, and cases requiring review.
- View confirmed paid amounts and outstanding balances.
- Export a reconciliation report containing match decisions, explanations, and unresolved records.

Include a labeled sample dataset and demonstrate correct matches, ambiguous cases, unmatched records, and safeguards against counting a payment twice.

### Expected Outcome

A transparent, reviewable reconciliation workflow that helps a small business understand its payment status and investigate discrepancies. Teams should report matching precision against the labeled dataset, the proportion of records receiving correct suggestions, and the number of unresolved cases. Successful solutions should maintain accurate balances, explain their recommendations, and preserve user corrections.
